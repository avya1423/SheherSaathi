Privacy & Legal Policy

Transparency on how your local data is secured and managed.

Data Storage Framework

SheherSaathi strictly uses Client-Side LocalStorage. Your expense trackers, saved PGs, and login session names never leave your physical device. We do not maintain centralized cloud tracking databases for user activity.

Property Listing Guidelines

  • Adding standard listings is permanently free.
  • Submitting fraudulent property images or spoofed numbers will result in network blacklisting.
  • Always physically verify the flat/PG before releasing advance security deposits.

User Rights & Deletion

You have total control. To erase all your interactions, budgets, and saved data, simply click the "Reset Ledger" button in the app or clear your browser's LocalStorage cache. No server-side deletion request is required.
