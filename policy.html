Privacy & Legal Policy

Transparency on how your data is secured and managed.

Last Updated: June 2026

Data Storage Framework

SheherSaathi strictly uses Client-Side LocalStorage. Your expense trackers, saved PGs, checklist progress, and login session information never leave your physical device. We do not maintain any remote servers storing your personal information. All data remains 100% encrypted on your browser.

Property Listing Guidelines

  • Free Listings: Adding standard PG/flat listings is permanently free. No hidden charges or surprise fees.
  • Fraud Prevention: Submitting fraudulent property images or spoofed contact numbers results in immediate network blacklisting and IP ban.
  • Safety First: Always physically verify the flat/PG before releasing advance security deposits. Meet the landlord/owner in person.
  • Verification Badge: Only listings verified by our team receive the "Verified" badge.

User Rights & Data Deletion

You have total control over your data. To erase all your interactions, budgets, saved accommodations, and session data, simply:

  1. 1. Click the "Reset Ledger" button in the app's Expense section, OR
  2. 2. Clear your browser's LocalStorage cache manually

No confirmation emails needed. Your data is instantly purged from all systems. This action is irreversible.

Emergency Helpline Privacy

All emergency support communications are confidential. We comply with national safety protocols. Information shared during emergencies is NOT stored in your LocalStorage and is handled by authorized support personnel only.

Cookies & Tracking

SheherSaathi does NOT use any third-party analytics, advertisement trackers, or cookie-based surveillance. Your browsing activity on SheherSaathi is completely private and is never shared with marketing companies or data brokers.

Liability Disclaimer

SheherSaathi is a platform for accommodation discovery and student utilities. While we verify listings, users should:

  • • Conduct independent background checks on property owners
  • • Never share OTP codes or bank details during initial contact
  • • Use secure payment methods (never cash advances)
  • • Report fraudulent listings immediately

SheherSaathi assumes no liability for disputes, scams, or property-related conflicts. All transactions are user's responsibility.
